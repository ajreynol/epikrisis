Two facts are visible only when the columns are read together. The tree holding 52.5% of the ecosystem's entire recorded history contributed nothing to the current stretch — logos's last commit lands four hours and fifty-four minutes before the term opened. And the tree holding the governance is the busiest tree in the stretch by a factor of three.

How many commits are AI-generated is not measurable, by anybody. What is measurable is disclosure: five commits out of 1,355 have ever carried an agent trailer, and zero of the 221 in Stretch 2 do — in any of the ten trees, or in ethos, which is outside the corpus and has 22 in its history. A trailer is opt-in, so the floor is zero and there is no ceiling.

The president's own measure

interim, and the stretch has not closed

The office was accepted with a falsifiable commitment attached: at the close of Stretch 2, docs/ must not have grown relative to the code it governs — against a baseline recorded in place, at anoieu 579aae7.

The baseline re-derives. 1,503 KiB of markdown against 581 KiB of Python, with docs/ alone at 1,066 KiB. That matches the figures written down at the time.

The measure is currently being met — and not by restraint. The governance moved trees mid-measure, so the honest reading follows the documents rather than the tree they left.

What the stress test broke

nine findings, six of them new

The run was built to be hard: ten sources instead of five, a corpus twice the size of any previous run, a labelled run stamp, and a subject whose source list is derived from the register rather than hand-written. Everything below was found by running it.

The pipeline has no window, so the census is a hand count

newblocks the ask

No stage takes a --since. Every stretch-scoped figure on this page — the census column, the prose-to-code measure, the disclosure count, the working-summary spacing — was produced with git by hand. The run directory is real; the number inside it is not a pipeline product.

A stale source list costs four times more on a stretch than on a history

newsilently wrong

The seven-source subject reports 1,298 of 1,355 commits — 4.2% short — which is the size the declaration implies. Over Stretch 2 the same subject reports 177 of 221, 19.9% short, because the three unread trees are the three newest. A stale denominator under-reports exactly what is new, and the whole-history view hides it.

Three defects in the run machinery, all found by using it

newsilent

pin --date writes the run stamp into the pinned field, so panel raises ValueError on any labelled run — six of the twelve already committed here. --date is advertised on every stage and honoured only by pin, so a later stage writes into whatever directory sorts last, silently. And "newest run" is sorted()[-1]: for anoieu that resolves to 2026-09-02b, not the most recent.

Seven trees stopped at once, and no claim accounts for it

newderived, not declared

Every repository then in existence went quiet between 2026-09-01/02 and 2026-09-14/16 — seven independent quiet candidates, each against a median inter-commit gap of about one day. Independent projects do not do that. None of the 169 extracted claims mentions the period.

Five things worth measuring next

ideas, not commitments

Each of these is derivable from git alone, needs no network, and answers a question somebody in this ecosystem has already asked in prose.

A window, and the stretch as a first-class object

One --since/--until on pin, plus a stretch stanza in the subject that names the opening commit. Every existing measure — ratio, panel, recent, ai-attribution — becomes stretch-scoped for free.

Turns the census from a hand count into output, and makes the president's docs-flat measure a command rather than an argument.

Derive the source list from the register, always

This run's subject was generated from ecosystem.json by a rule (status in member, president) rather than typed. Make that the only way a subject exists, and the denominator cannot fall behind.

The 19.9% stretch-window error above disappears by construction instead of by remembering.

Follow the register across repositories

The register's joined field already names the repository and commit that held the policy at each join, precisely because the policy moves. Read it, and reconstruct the transitions the handoff truncated.

Recovers seven missing join events and stops one afternoon looking like the ecosystem's origin.

Retire the matcher, or give it identifiers

Three runs, three voids, and the collision count rising with corpus size. Either claims carry explicit event identifiers, or the delta stops reporting a match count that reads as progress.

The catalogue's own rule is that a detector whose candidates are always dropped is deleted rather than tuned.

A concentration measure, per stretch

Not a new detector — one line over what pin already holds: what share of a stretch's commits landed in one tree. It reads 38% for kanon this stretch, against 0% for logos.

It is the figure that makes "the governance layer has outgrown what it governs" checkable rather than arguable.

Advice

not part of the report — a report may not say what to do next

The judgement rules forbid a recommendation inside a report; every claim there is about what happened. So the advice sits here, separately, and is a person's to act on or discard.

Ship the window before shipping another census

to epikrisis

The duty accepted in D4 was per-tool commit counts for a stretch. What exists is per-tool commit counts for all time, plus a hand count beside it. Publishing the second while it is labelled as the first is the failure this project named for itself. A window is the smallest change that closes the gap, and it is smaller than the four defects found this week.

Do not let the denominator be a thing anybody remembers

to epikrisis

The gap was declared honestly in not_in_corpus and it still produced a number 19.9% short on the window anybody would actually read. A declared narrowness is not a fix. Generate the source list; delete the ability to type one.

The figure is available now, and the caveat travels with it

to kanon

Commits per tool for Stretch 2 exist and are on this page. Two things must travel with them every time: the count is hand-read rather than pipeline-derived, and the share of commits believed AI-generated is not measurable by anybody — five disclosures across 1,355 commits, none in this stretch. A required field nobody can measure stays a standing admission, not an invitation to estimate.

The docs-flat measure needs its scope fixed before the stretch closes

to kanon

Its baseline is in anoieu and the documents have since moved to kanon, so the measure currently reads 1.03, 2.19 or 7.83 depending on which tree you point it at. Deciding that after seeing the numbers is the fitted-threshold problem in prose form. Name the reading now — following the documents is the defensible one — and state it either way at close, as promised.

Record that logos was silent for the whole term

to kanon

Not as a criticism — a mature artifact going quiet for three days is nothing. But 52.5% of the ecosystem's recorded history produced zero commits while 38% of the term's work went into governance, and a term record that omits it is the reconstruction problem the record exists to prevent. It is cheap to write down now and expensive to notice later.

to a person
